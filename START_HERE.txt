WHAT THIS PROJECT DOES:
-----------------------
Automatically trims 10 songs to specific timestamps and saves them with 
custom names (ui.mp3, admin.mp3, backend.mp3, etc.)

QUICK START (3 STEPS):
----------------------

STEP 1: Check Status
--------------------
Run this command to see what's needed:

    python3 check_status.py


STEP 2: Download Songs
----------------------
Download these 10 songs as MP3 files and rename them:

    1. palat.mp3
    2. radhe_radhe.mp3
    3. arjun_valley.mp3
    4. shanaya.mp3
    5. karvaan.mp3
    6. maine_tujhe_dekha.mp3
    7. faasla.mp3
    8. sauda_sauda.mp3
    9. galti_se_mistake.mp3
    10. paisa_h_toh.mp3

Place all files in: /vercel/sandbox/source_mp3/


STEP 3: Run Trimming Script
----------------------------
Once all files are in place, run:

    python3 trim_existing_mp3.py

Your trimmed files will appear in: /vercel/sandbox/trimmed_mp3/


OUTPUT FILES:
-------------
You will get 10 trimmed MP3 files:

    ui.mp3          (from palat, 0:26-1:03)
    admin.mp3       (from radhe_radhe, 0:11-0:37)
    backend.mp3     (from arjun_valley, 0:00-0:30)
    hr.mp3          (from shanaya, 0:00-0:48)
    migration.mp3   (from karvaan, 1:06-1:31)
    qa.mp3          (from maine_tujhe_dekha, 1:05-1:22)
    arun.mp3        (from faasla, 0:00-0:18 + 0:36-0:57)
    marketing.mp3   (from sauda_sauda, 0:12-0:19 + 0:50-1:33)
    ai.mp3          (from galti_se_mistake, 0:09-0:51)
    finance.mp3     (from paisa_h_toh, 0:46-1:19)
